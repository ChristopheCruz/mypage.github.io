    Christophe Cruz¶

    Full Professor of Computer Science · IUT de Dijon · ICB Laboratory UMR CNRS 6303 · Université Bourgogne Europe

    I design AI architectures able to reason, adapt and interact naturally with humans, at the intersection of knowledge modelling, generative AI and agentic systems.

    My research Publications Contact

    Research topics¶

    • Generative AI and LLMs

      Summarisation, classification and strategic intelligence in demanding business settings.

    • Neuro-symbolic AI

      Knowledge graphs and generative models for reliable, explainable results.

    • RAG and knowledge graphs

      Grounding LLMs in structured, evolving knowledge bases.

    • AI agents

      Multimodal, empathetic and adaptive agents, within the LAMAE LabCom.

    • Cognitive Network Science

      Modelling cognition with networks to better understand human-AI interaction.
